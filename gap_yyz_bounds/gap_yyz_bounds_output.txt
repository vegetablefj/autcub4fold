YYZ / Laza--Zheng group-theoretic audit for cubic fourfold automorphisms
======================================================================

Background (brief).
Yang--Yu--Zhu classify finite groups acting faithfully on smooth cubic
fourfolds by showing that every such group is isomorphic to a subgroup
of one of 15 maximal groups.  Laza--Zheng classify the 34 possible
symplectic automorphism groups.  In the index calculation used here,
Aut^s(X) is normal in Aut(X), the quotient is cyclic, and the admissible
index m is required to be 2^a or 3*2^a.

The calculation below is group-theoretic: a listed group is a necessary
candidate inside the YYZ maximal groups, not by itself a geometric
realizability statement.  Conversely, a zero count below means that the
script searched all subgroup conjugacy classes of all 15 YYZ maximal
groups and found no subgroup satisfying the stated normal/cyclic-quotient
conditions for that index.
