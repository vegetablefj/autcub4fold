# Nos. 117 and 130 inside the saved No. 95 action

`restriction_117_130_geometric_characters.g` reads the frozen numbered six-dimensional groups and their direct strict GL(6) containment witnesses. It checks the saved conjugations into No. 95 and writes the exact projective primitive-\(H^4\) character bins. It refuses to replace an existing TSV.

`restriction_117_130_from_95.jl` starts from the assigned full integral action of No. 95. Its normal symplectic kernel is \(N\cong S_3\) and its quotient is \(C_{12}\). It builds all 72 distinct matrices \(nf^k\), an exact multiplication table, and checks associativity and matrix products. The subgroup search is exhaustive within this saved parent:

| Child | Kernel intersection | Quotient | Enumerated lifts |
|---|---|---|---|
| No. 117, `[18,3]` | the unique \(C_3\) in \(N\) | \(C_6\) | every element of \(f^2N\) |
| No. 130, `[24,9]` | each \(C_2\) in \(N\) | \(C_{12}\) | every element of \(fN\) |

For either child, any eligible subgroup is generated by its kernel intersection and a lift of the quotient generator. The script takes exact subgroup closures, checks their SmallGroup IDs, and forms conjugacy orbits under the *whole* 72-element No. 95 group. It compares the full order/trace histogram with the GAP character, then computes the embedded \(S,T,P,K\), quotient action, and period dimension. The final stage reconstructs each retained restriction and checks that \(K\) has no root. A character match alone does not assign a numbered family; all matching parent classes are retained separately. Symplectic saturation and integral conjugacy between distinct matching classes are not claimed.

Run from `remark/catalogue`, sequentially with one Julia thread:

```text
GAP:   Read("restriction_117_130_geometric_characters.g");
Julia: restriction_117_130_from_95.jl preflight
Julia: restriction_117_130_from_95.jl groups
Julia: restriction_117_130_from_95.jl lattices
Julia: restriction_117_130_from_95.jl verify
```

The `lattices` stage optionally accepts `[output.mrdi] [groups.mrdi] [117:class or 130:class]` for a separate smoke-test file. `groups` and `verify` also accept explicit output paths; `verify` can additionally take lattice and group input paths. Every Julia stage refuses to overwrite its output and checks its inputs and implementation. The completed outputs are `restriction_117_130_from_95.groups.mrdi`, `.lattices.mrdi`, and `.verified.mrdi`.

Each target has exactly one parent-conjugacy class with the required abstract group and exact geometric character. Both classes pass the full embedded lattice, quotient-action, period-dimension, and root-obstruction checks. The final record was saved and reloaded with verified counts `(1,1)` and no root-obstructed classes. The calculation checks the No. 95 parent action, numbered table, strict-containment inputs, geometric-character files, and lattice code. The result identifies these restrictions within the saved No. 95 action; it does not enumerate every integral action outside that parent, independently compute the discriminant-kernel full-group ID, or independently check symplectic saturation.
