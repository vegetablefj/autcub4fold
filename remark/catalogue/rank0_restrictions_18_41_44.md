# Rank-zero cyclic restrictions from Nos. 18, 41 and 44

The earlier rank-zero calculation read the frozen
`../../oscar/oscar_script_data.mrdi`.
The selected rank-22 full lattice actions are No. 18 = case 6/result 1,
No. 41 = case 16/result 3, and No. 44 = case 18/result 1. No. 18 has a
second saved auxiliary output; the existing auxiliary-equivalence audit
puts the two outputs in one lattice-theoretic class, but this script uses
only the numbered catalogue's selected result 1.

| Parent | Saved symplectic group | `rank(S)` | Quotient order `q` | Saved full group ID | Expected `|N|` |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 18 | `M_9` | 19 | 3 | `[216,153]` | 72 |
| 41 | `F_21` | 18 | 6 | `[126,7]` | 21 |
| 44 | `QD_16` | 18 | 2 | `[32,42]` | 16 |

Here `N` is the kernel of the action of `O(S)` on the discriminant group.
The saved rank-22 extra generator is `f`, and its action on the parent's
invariant `T` has exact order `q`. The script checks every stated source
index, order, group ID, rank, signature and discriminant before using the
result. The target table rows in `../input/family_numbering.md`
are No. 134 (order 2, dimension 10), No. 136 (order 3, dimension 7),
No. 140 (order 3, dimension 6), and No. 143 (order 6, dimension 3).

The default `powers` stage tests `f^(q/m)` for each target order `m` dividing
`q`. For each action it computes the exact primitive rank-22 trace of every
power, the saturated `Phi_m` eigenlattice `P`, `K=P^perp`, and the period
dimension. It writes a Markdown diagnostic report without editing the
156-row catalogue. This stage can miss cyclic subgroups generated by
`n*f^(q/m)` with nonidentity `n` in `N`.

A read-only, subsecond integer-matrix check of the serialized ambient
isometries gives trace vectors `[tr(f^0),...,tr(f^(q-1))]` equal to
`[22,-2,-2]` for No. 18, `[22,1,1,-2,1,1]` for No. 41, and `[22,-2]`
for No. 44. It also verifies `f^q=1` in each case. Cyclotomic trace
identities give the following rational eigenspace ranks and period
dimensions. The subsequent OSCAR `powers` run confirmed the integral `P`
and `K` records in `rank0_pure_powers_18_41_44_20261001.md`.

| Parent power | Order | `rank(P)` | Period dimension | Exact geometric character match |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| No. 18 `f` | 3 | 16 | 7 | No. 136 |
| No. 41 `f^3` | 2 | 12 | 10 | No. 134 |
| No. 41 `f^2` | 3 | 14 | 6 | No. 140 |

The matches use the full cyclic order-trace fingerprint of each saved
geometric representative, rather than period dimension alone. No. 134
matches pure powers in two different parents, so even exact character
equality does not settle the integral action or parent-class assignment.
The separate No. 127 involution pilot is not compared here either.

The explicit `prepare` stage reconstructs the full parent lattice group.
It computes `N` from the embedded `S` lattice, extends its generators to
the saved rank-22 lattice, and saves a separate parent cache. This can be
the expensive OSCAR step. The `enumerate` stage reads the cache, checks
the extension relations in both the `S` and rank-22 actions, and solves

`(n*f^(q/m))^m = n*phi^(q/m)(n)*...*phi^((m-1)q/m)(n)*f^q = 1`,

where `phi` is conjugation by `f` on `N`. Every solution has exact order
`m`, because its image in the quotient has order `m`. The solutions are
partitioned by conjugation under all generators of the parent group. A
cyclic subgroup with this quotient image has exactly one generator
projecting to the fixed quotient generator `f^(q/m)`, so this partitions
subgroups without generator duplicates. Each class representative is
lifted back to the rank-22 action and checked by exact matrix powers,
integral isometry, trivial discriminant action, period signature, and
rank-22 trace histogram. The saved MRDI record contains its full ambient
restriction, `rank_S=0`, `T=Lambda0`, `P`, `K`, and the relevant actions.
It does not serialize a separate zero-dimensional isometry object.

The default tab-separated geometric character file is
`../gap_checks/cyclic_134_143/primitive_cyclic_134_143_summary.tsv`.
Its construction is documented in that directory's `README.md`; exact
form-fixing cubic witnesses and smoothness checks support the trace
calculation. The file has columns
`family_number`, `projective_order`, `primitive_H4_trace`, and
`element_count`, with one row per order-trace pair. The script records
exact histogram matches, separately
from the order-and-dimension matches. It deliberately leaves
`numbered_assignments_verified=false`: equal complex characters need not
give the same integral lattice action, and neither a period dimension nor
a group order alone identifies a numbered family. The independent root
test is also outside this script. The original source search rejected
parent outputs with short or long roots in `K` (`oscar/oscar_script.jl`,
`lattice_data_after_root_and_symplectic_tests`), and the
period-map/Torelli construction realizes the entire parent group
`<N,f>` on one smooth cubic under the stable root-free realization
criterion. Thus the enumerated
elements are geometric on that common parent cubic, conditional on the
retained source certification. This bridge does not itself identify their
integral actions with numbered child equations. The outputs remain
candidate numbered restrictions, not a new global classification.

The three saved outputs are `rank0_cyclic_classes_from_18.mrdi`,
`rank0_cyclic_classes_from_41.mrdi`, and
`rank0_cyclic_classes_from_44.mrdi`. The No. 18
enumeration has two order-three classes, uniquely matching the No. 136
and No. 140 geometric characters within that parent. No. 44 has one
No. 134 character match among two order-two classes. No. 41 has one
order-two class and three each of orders three and six; all three classes
at the latter orders match the corresponding No. 140 or No. 143 character.
