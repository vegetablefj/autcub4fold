## Coordinate actions and cubic spaces

### No. 96

In the readable `S3` coordinates, take

```text
A = diag(zeta8, -zeta4, -1, 1, omega, 1),
B = (5 6).
```

The projective group generated by `A,B` is `S3 x C24`; adjoining
`omega*I6` gives the strict inverse image.  A basis of the invariant cubic
space is

```text
x1^2*x2,
x2^2*x3,
x3^2*x4,
x4^3,
x5^3 + x6^3.
```

The isolated smooth member used in the source is their sum.

### No. 127

In the standard `C2` coordinates, the non-symplectic generator and
symplectic involution are

```text
A = diag(zeta4, -1, 1, 1, 1, -1),
s = diag(1, 1, 1, 1, -1, -1).
```

Together with `omega*I6`, these give the strict group.  A basis of the
twelve-dimensional invariant space is

```text
x1^2*x2,
x2^2*x3, x2^2*x4, x2*x5*x6,
x3^3, x3^2*x4, x3*x4^2, x3*x5^2, x3*x6^2,
x4^3, x4*x5^2, x4*x6^2.
```

These are `P_2,P_15,P_16,P_21,P_23,P_24,P_25,P_26,P_28,P_29,P_30,P_32`
in the local standard `C2` basis.

### No. 155



Its general smooth member is one projective-equivalence class; the displayed

## Scope of the result

This calculation fixes the exact rational characteristic and signature
constraints to feed into the integral lattice search.  It also distinguishes
No. 152 from No. 151 and No. 154 from No. 153, despite their matching coarse
group data.  It does not yet prove that the resulting integral action is
unique up to `O(T)`-conjugacy.  That requires the subsequent OSCAR genus,
gluing, root, and stable-ambient checks.
