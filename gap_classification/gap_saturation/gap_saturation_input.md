# Unified saturation input

`gap_saturation_input.g` only reads and normalizes data. It does not run an
embedding or saturation search.

## Input

| source | smooth | unresolved | total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| rank of `S` at least 15 | 76 | 0 | 76 |
| liftable abelian | 53 | 0 | 53 |
| non-liftable abelian | 2 | 0 | 2 |
| small non-abelian | 46 | 7 | 53 |
| **total** | **177** | **7** | **184** |

The 129 small non-abelian families with a family-wide singularity
certificate are omitted. The small non-abelian input is generated by
`gap_prepare_small_nonabelian_input.g`; its header records the input filenames
and the smooth, unresolved, and excluded counts. The supplied records retain
the exact generators, bases, and source labels. If either input is replaced
in a future run, the selected records must be compared individually as part
of a separate data release; equal counts alone do not establish agreement.

## Tags

Each normalized record has one `saturationTag`:

- `"known_saturated"`: no saturation test is needed;
- `"check"`: test it once smoothness is known.

There are 60 known saturated families:

- 48 connected symplectic families;
- the five proper families covered by the rank-at-least-19 or
  group-order-divisible-by-7 criterion;
- `C32` and `C48`;
- the three index-4, index-6, and index-12 families with symplectic part
  `3^{1+4}:2`;
- `S3 x C24`;
- the index-2 family with symplectic part `QD16`.

Thus `48+5+2+3+1+1=60`. Hence the 177 smooth records consist of 60
permanent targets and 117 records eligible for the saturation search. The
seven unresolved records remain loaded but are not eligible.

The tags encode mathematical input, not a successful outcome of the
containment program. The sources are listed in the
[reference guide](../../REFERENCES.md#symplectic-actions-and-maximal-groups):

- the 48 connected-family records are their generic full stabilizers;
- the five rank/order records are the two proper `F21` rows, the two proper
  `L2(7)` rows, and the proper `M9` row, covered by the order-7 and rank-19
  classifications;
- the `QD16` row contains Yang–Yu–Zhu's cubic `X'_12`, whose full group is
  exactly `(C8 x C2):C2` and whose symplectic group is `QD16`. The displayed
  order-32 group acts on the whole row, while stabilizer order can only jump
  on a proper closed locus. This exact member therefore determines the
  general stabilizer;
- the three proper `3^{1+4}:2` rows follow from
  [uniqueness of maximal additive splitting](../../REFERENCES.md#additive-splittings)
  and Yang–Yu–Zhu's plane-cubic stabilizer classification;
- `C32`, `C48`, and `S3 x C24` are the explicit full groups computed in the
  first non-symplectic-index paper from the Yang–Yu–Zhu examples.

The equal-dimensional search was completed before the separate
self-conjugacy audit. This does not affect the saturation result. Complex
conjugation preserves strict containment and family dimension, and none of
the 27 tested large-family records was removed. Self-conjugacy is used only
to show that the two possible type-IV components give one equation-family
row.

The principal lists are:

```gap
SaturationInputCandidates;
SaturationInputKnownSaturated;
SaturationInputForSaturationCheck;
```

The four sources and the unresolved part also remain separately available:

```gap
SaturationInputLargeFamilies;
SaturationInputLiftableAbelian;
SaturationInputNonliftableAbelian;
SaturationInputSmallNonabelianSmooth;
SaturationInputSmallNonabelianUnresolved;
```

Every record retains the source, family dimension, symplectic part, full
index, GL and PGL IDs, matrix generators, and ordered invariant basis. The
descriptive `sourceKey` does not use an earlier catalogue number.

`genericIndex` records the generic index of the connected symplectic
family, while `fullIndex` records the candidate's index `[G:G_s]`.
For both non-liftable abelian inputs, `genericIndex = 1`; their full
indices are 3 and 6. The loader verifies the supplied `fullIndex`
against the determinant image order. These two records remain tagged
`"check"`; their source keys and saturation decisions are unchanged.

## Loading

From the repository root:

```gap
Read("gap_classification/gap_saturation/gap_saturation_input.g");
SaturationInputSummary;
```

The file can also be read from inside `gap_classification/gap_saturation`.
